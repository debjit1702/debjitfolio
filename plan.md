## Design direction

Three tail-sampled alternatives considered: museum-grade neobrutalism 0.08; alpine editorial minimalism 0.06; **spatial UI with playful neo-grotesk editorial craft 0.04 (selected)**. The user’s supplied reel screenshot and palette guide the art direction.

- **Design Movement:** Spatial UI rendered as playful editorial software-portfolio design.
- **Core Principles:** Clear functional hierarchy; tactile depth without clutter; prominent color blocks with restrained typography; responsive layouts that retain designed asymmetry.
- **Color Philosophy:** Lavender is the canvas for imagination, chartreuse signals active edges and calls to action, and tomato red adds energetic punctuation. Warm paper and deep ink preserve light-mode contrast; a charcoal night palette keeps the same three accents legible in dark mode.
- **Layout Paradigm:** A wide left-aligned editorial hero paired with a current-role card, past-role tile and animated technology-logo marquee, then an About photo marquee and staggered content sections; a responsive six-card gallery for project discovery and an expanded multi-column footer.
- **Signature Elements:** A lavender current-role card, a chartreuse past-internship tile and an icon-led technology ticker; an energetic strip of personal photos; layered, rounded project-cover art; small locator chips and live Kolkata time.
- **Reference Influence:** Use https://ui.watermelon.sh/home as a broad component-catalog reference and https://ui.watermelon.sh/blocks/footer as inspiration for a complete contact-led footer. Follow the user's Magic UI marquee reference at https://magicui.design/docs/components/marquee for repeated horizontal tracks, reverse direction and pause-on-hover behavior; use the locally implemented marquee for both photo and technology strips instead of adding a component dependency. Use React Icons for recognized brand marks, with simple neutral symbols for generative-AI concepts. The implementation remains original and uses this portfolio’s own routes, links, palette and typography.
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Direct and predictable navigation with a subtle synthesized click accent, reduced-motion support, an explicit sound toggle and a persistent site-wide light/dark switch.
- **Animation:** Short, low-amplitude entrance and hover transitions with a seamless, moderate-speed technology ticker and photo marquee; pause on hover/focus and stop motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Typography System:** Space Grotesk for display headlines; Inter/system sans for body and UI; compact uppercase mono labels for metadata.
- **Brand Essence:** A Kolkata full-stack engineer showing the work and the working parts; curious, inventive, practical.
- **Brand Voice:** Playful, precise and unpretentious. Example: “Ideas go in. Useful software comes out.” “Still iterating; never just decorating.”
- **Wordmark & Logo:** A custom compact `db/` monogram inspired by code braces and an orbital chartreuse accent.
- **Signature Brand Color:** Electric lavender `#B596F4`, balanced by chartreuse `#D5FA48` and tomato red `#ED4E3D`.
